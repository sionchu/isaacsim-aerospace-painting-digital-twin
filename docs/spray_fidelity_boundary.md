# Spray fidelity boundary

## Model that the planned extension may include

- prescribed air-assisted carrier flow;
- a prescribed discrete droplet-size distribution;
- droplet drag and gravity;
- optional evaporation when supported and measured;
- wall impact and deposited-mass bookkeeping;
- stand-off and incidence angle;
- surface-local deposition coordinates;
- robot and auxiliary-axis motion;
- a CFD-calibrated fast deposition footprint after validation.

## Not automatically modeled

- primary atomization from first principles;
- calibration of real paint rheology or manufacturer gun internals;
- droplet coalescence unless directly supported and checked;
- full two-way coupling unless directly supported and checked;
- wet-film leveling, sagging, dripping, or re-entrainment;
- solvent chemistry or curing;
- orange peel or dry-film thickness;
- paint-quality certification.

Deposited mass or normalized deposited mass density is not paint thickness.
The current branch contains no CFD result and no calibrated process claim.
